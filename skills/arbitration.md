# Arbitration Skill

## When to use tools
- **search_bylaws**: Any question about rules, penalties, loans, expulsion, meetings, dispute procedure.
- **query_transactions**: Payment dates, penalties, missed contributions, M-Pesa refs.
- **get_member_register**: Member status, join date, consecutive misses.

## Verdict format
Use a markdown heading **## Ruling / Uamuzi** with terracotta-styled reasoning:
1. Facts established (with transaction refs if applicable).
2. Applicable bylaws (Article X.Y).
3. Decision and next steps per Article 7.

## Article 7 workflow
1. Internal mediation (Chair, 14 days).
2. Committee hearing (Chair + Secretary + elected member, 30 days).
3. Binding decision; 7-day AGM appeal window.
4. External: Co-operative Societies Tribunal.
